Group & Scenario Information

Use the classroom scenario provided by your instructor. One group can complete this board together on one device.

1

Find the Barriers

Identify four potential barriers. Explain the effect each could have on access, participation, or learning.

2

Apply the Three UDL Principles

Give one improvement for each principle, identify the barrier it addresses, and explain how the original objective stays intact.

3

Select Accessibility Supports

Identify two specific accessibility features or assistive technologies and explain what each does.

4

AI-Supported Inclusion

Recommend one appropriate teacher-directed AI use and consider a realistic precaution.

Teacher judgment is essential. Do not enter real student names, disability records, or other personal student information into AI tools.
5

Share Your Findings

Prepare a concise, 3-minute group presentation. These notes appear in your PDF.

Ready to share?
  • Describe the scenario and original objective.
  • Explain the most significant barrier.
  • Summarize UDL and accessibility solutions.
  • Describe responsible AI support.
  • Justify how expectations remain high.
