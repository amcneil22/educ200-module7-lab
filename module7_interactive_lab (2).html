Learn the Three UDL Principles

Universal Design for Learning encourages teachers to plan for learner variability instead of waiting until barriers prevent participation. These principles work together; a strategy can support more than one.

WHY

Engagement

Offer purposeful ways to sustain motivation and participation, such as relevant choices, collaboration, and belonging.

Example: Learners choose a local issue to investigate.

WHAT

Representation

Provide accessible ways to perceive and understand content, such as accurate captions, diagrams with text equivalents, and vocabulary supports.

Example: A science concept is explained with text, a captioned video, and an accessible diagram.

HOW

Action & Expression

Offer appropriate ways to respond and demonstrate understanding while preserving the learning objective.

Example: Students explain a process through writing or a narrated diagram when the goal is conceptual understanding.

Think like a teacher: If the learning objective is to write a persuasive paragraph, should students replace writing entirely with an oral response? Not necessarily—the assessment must still measure the targeted writing skill.

Redesign a Lesson

Learning objective: Fifth-grade students will accurately explain evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.

BEFORE: Traditional Lesson

The teacher assigns a video without captions, a dense reading passage, and the same handwritten worksheet for every student.

Make three instructional decisions

AFTER: Inclusive Lesson

Engagement: Students connect the water cycle to local weather and choose a relevant inquiry question.

Representation: Provide an accurate captioned video, accessible diagram with equivalent text, and optional read-aloud support.

Action & Expression: Students explain all four processes through an accessible format that measures the same science objective.

Responsible AI: The teacher may use an approved AI tool to draft a vocabulary scaffold, verify the scientific accuracy, and avoid entering personal student information.

Assessment: Use the same content-based success criteria for all permitted response formats.
